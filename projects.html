Our Work

Properties around Pine Mountain Lake, Groveland and Big Oak Flat. Here is what each kind of job involves, start to finish.

Straight answer

Photographs are coming

Bryan is putting together a set of his own work to show here. Until then, the drawings below are an honest account of how each kind of job actually goes — what he does, in what order, and what you are left with.

Decks and painting

Stripped back and brought home

A mountain deck or a tired room, taken from worn out to looked after.

  • Everything moved out or masked up before a brush goes near it
  • The old finish taken back properly, not painted over
  • Two coats, cut in by hand where it matters
  • Sheets up, brushes washed, and the room put back as it was
Fire-safe clearing

The work that changes how a property fares

Defensible space done the way the fire service asks for it, zone by zone.

  • Needles and leaf litter off the roof, out of the gutters, away from the walls
  • Low limbs raised so a ground fire cannot climb
  • Brush thinned and spaced instead of scalped
  • Everything cut is hauled off, not left in a pile
Yard waste

The pile behind the fence, gone

Storm droppings, needles, cones and cuttings — loaded and taken away the same visit.

  • Raked, gathered and bagged or barrowed
  • Loaded by hand, so beds and gravel are not churned up
  • Taken to the proper facility, receipt kept
  • The ground swept down before he leaves
Junk and debris

Cleared out and taken away

Garages, sheds, and the things too big or too awkward for the truck.

  • Appliances, furniture, construction leftovers, general clear-outs
  • The lifting and the loading are his, not yours
  • Anything that can be donated or recycled goes that way first
  • A disposal fee is part of the written price, never a surprise
Property watch

Eyes on the place while you are away

For second homes: a regular look round, and a straight answer about what he found.

  • A walk round inside and out, to an agreed checklist
  • Photos of anything that needs your decision
  • Small things put right on the spot where they can be
  • A message the same day, whether or not there is anything to report
