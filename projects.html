Bryan Wainwright is not a licensed contractor. Under California law (Business & Professions Code §7027.2 and §7048) he takes on jobs where the total for labour, materials and everything else comes to less than $1,000, and that do not need a building permit. Bigger jobs, and anything needing a permit, go to a licensed contractor — he will say so and often knows who to call.
