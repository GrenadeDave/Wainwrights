Before you call

Two minutes that make the visit better

  • Jot down everything on the list, even the small things — grouping jobs into one visit is cheaper than three separate ones.
  • Take a couple of photos on your phone if the job is outside; Bryan can often tell you a lot before he arrives.
  • Worried about fire clearance? Run through the defensible space checklist first and send him the result.
  • If you are arranging this for a parent or a relative, say so — Bryan is used to keeping family in the loop.
